THE STUDIO

An independent creative studio for small businesses finding their feet or finding a new direction.

Starting a business takes a lot of decisions. So does deciding it’s time for a change. We help with the visual side: how your business looks, how it introduces itself, and how it comes together.

That might start with a logo, colors, and type. It might mean a website, packaging, or a fresh look at what you already have. We shape the work around what your business needs.

The aim is simple: design that feels right for your business, makes sense to your customers, and gives you the confidence to put it out there.

HOW WE THINK

First, we listen.
Then we work out what needs to change.

We want to know what you’re building, who it’s for, and where you’re getting stuck. You can bring a rough idea, an existing business, or a list of questions.

We’ll help you choose a direction and explain the thinking behind the design. The goal is to make the next step clearer, with work you understand and feel good about using.

That's what we mean by ideas first, design second.
