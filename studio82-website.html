THE DIRECTION

Make the work speak.
Make the next step clear.

We designed Studio82’s website to introduce the studio with the same clarity and character we bring to client work. A dark canvas, oversized typography, and bright cyan accents give the site a distinctive voice while leaving room for the projects to stand out.

The content speaks to small businesses starting something new, finding a fresh direction, or planning their next chapter. Build, Reset, and Grow organize the services around those needs, with clear routes to the portfolio and contact page.

DESIGN INTO DEVELOPMENT

A home for the studio’s work.

A consistent identity.

Typography, color, spacing, and shared navigation connect the homepage, about page, contact page, and project stories.

Projects with context.

Image-led portfolio cards lead into case studies that explain the ideas, visual choices, and work behind each project.

Built for exploration.

Responsive HTML and CSS layouts adapt to different screen sizes. JavaScript adds pointer-responsive service cards, while direct contact links keep enquiries within reach.
