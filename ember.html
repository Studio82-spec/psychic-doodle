COFFEE BUILT
AROUND RITUAL.

EMBER is a conceptual coffee brand built around the feeling of warmth, focus, and the everyday ritual of preparing coffee.

The identity combines bold typography, warm tones, and confident packaging to create a brand that feels energetic without becoming chaotic.

The goal was to create a flexible visual system capable of moving across packaging, advertising, social content, and future campaign applications without losing its personality.

BRAND SYSTEM

BUILT TO LIVE
BEYOND THE BAG.

The EMBER identity was designed as a system rather than a single package, allowing the brand to extend into advertising, digital content, and campaign applications.

THE IDENTITY

A SYSTEM BUILT
FROM HEAT and FOCUS.

EMBER pairs bold, clean lettering with a simple flat flame. Orange identifies Dark Roast and blue identifies Decaf with Lions Mane, keeping both varieties part of one clear family.
