IMMERSA

Entertainment that takes you out of the everyday.

PROJECT
Concept exploration
FOCUS
Creative direction & campaign imagery
APPLICATIONS
Home cinema & live sports

THE IDEA

A familiar room.
An unexpected feeling.

IMMERSA imagines a home entertainment experience that feels bigger than the screen. The concept takes the feeling of being completely absorbed in a film or a game and makes it visible: the viewer, and the objects around them, lift off the ground.

A living room becomes the setting for something extraordinary. The floating device, suspended snacks, and weightless spectators connect both campaign images through one clear visual idea.

This is a speculative creative concept, exploring a fictional experience through branding and campaign imagery.

THE CAMPAIGN

Two moments.
One elevated experience.

VISUAL DIRECTION

Make the feeling visible.

Start with the familiar.

A sofa, a screen, and an evening at home give the viewer an immediate way into the idea. The ordinary setting makes the impossible detail stand out.

Give the idea a signature.

Weightlessness connects the images. The blue-lit device anchors the imagined experience, while bold IMMERSA lettering gives each poster a consistent sign-off.

Let the moment change.

Cinema feels absorbed and atmospheric. Sport feels expressive and energetic. The shared visual approach holds together while the mood follows the audience.
